Drop MD Privacy Policy

Drop MD is a Mac app by Eric Yardley Digital LLC.

The app converts files on your own Mac. It does not collect, store, or send personal data to Eric Yardley Digital LLC.

There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Purchases on the Mac App Store are handled by Apple. Apple’s privacy policy is at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Purchases on Gumroad are handled by Gumroad. Gumroad’s privacy policy is at https://gumroad.com/privacy.
